Biography

I am a MSc student in Astronomy at the University of São Paulo (USP), Brazil. My research focuses on the modeling of different non-thermal sources in the Galaxy. During my undergraduate studies, I received the FAPESP fellowship to work alongside Prof. Dr. Maria Victoria del Valle on the modeling of non-thermal emission from a variety of astrophysical sources, such as colliding wind binaries, Novae and High Velocity Clouds.

My Bachelor thesis explored how T Tauri stars can accelerate particles and produce high-energy non-thermal emission, potentially reducing the number of unidentified sources in the gamma-ray domain, as well as suggesting a new candidate for future observations with the next generation Cherenkov observatories such as the Cherenkov Telescope Array (CTA). For this thesis, I received my undergraduate degree in Astronomy from the University of São Paulo in 2024.

At USP I have also been involved in teaching and outreach activities. I was a teaching assistant for the undergraduate courses Introduction to Astronomy I in the first semesters of 2022 and 2024, and High Energy Astrophysics in the second semester of 2024. During the International Symposium of Scientific Initiation (SIICUSP) in 2023, I presented a flash talk about my research for the university community.

Currently, my research focuses on the multiwavelength modeling of protoplanetary nebulae as potential counterparts of unidentified gamma-ray sources detected by the Fermi-LAT.
